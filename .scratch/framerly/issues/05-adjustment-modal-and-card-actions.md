# 05: Adjustment Modal and Card Actions

**What to build:** Interactive manual adjustment modal and per-picture grid actions. Users can click any picture card in the Visual Grid to open a modal where they can drag-to-pan, zoom with a slider, rotate by 90-degree steps, or reset to auto-centered. Each card also provides a quick remove button and an instant single-picture download button.

**Blocked by:** 04: Auto-Centering and Crop Modes

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Clicking any card in the Visual Grid opens the Adjustment Modal with live interactive preview
- [ ] Direct click-and-drag panning within the frame aperture
- [ ] Smooth zoom slider allows zooming in and out of the picture
- [ ] 90-degree rotation button to fix sideways or upside-down pictures
- [ ] Reset button restores the picture to the original auto-centered state
- [ ] Individual picture cards feature a quick "Remove" button to discard unwanted or blurry photos
- [ ] Individual picture cards and modal feature a "Download Picture" button for instant single-image export
